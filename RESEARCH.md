# Practice mode research

The designs below are based on public descriptions from each product's own site or official app-store listing. They support interaction patterns for note-reading practice; they do not establish that any product is specifically intended for professionals, children, or only one age group.

| Product                                                                     | What its source explicitly describes                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Pattern adapted here                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [NoteWorks](https://doremiworld.com/Games/NoteWorks)                        | The developer describes the app for beginners through advanced students and users of all ages. It offers piano-key answers, A–G or Do–Re–Mi names, practice hints, adjustable speed, timed or untimed answers, and a note range that grows in the game. | Separate staff-name choices from piano-key answers.                                                                                                     |
| [Note Rush](https://www.noterushapp.com/)                                   | Its site calls the app a virtual flashcard deck for all ages. It listens to an instrument for answers, gives speed/accuracy feedback, lets players race the clock or hide the timer, and offers optional landmark-note hints.                           | Keep a flashcard path. Physical instrument or microphone input is not implemented in this update.                                                     |
| [Musicca](https://www.musicca.com/note-identification)                      | The site offers separate treble, bass, piano, and ear-training note-identification exercises; it says exercise series gradually increase in difficulty and can be practiced at the learner's own pace.                                                  | Offer discrete modes and a small default round, with an all-notes option.                                                                              |
| [Tenuto / musictheory.net](https://www.musictheory.net/exercises?More=)     | Official exercise descriptions separate staff-note identification from keyboard-note and keyboard-reverse identification; exercises can be customized.                                                                                                  | Make note naming and piano-key matching distinct answer modes.                                                                                         |
| [Complete Music Reading Trainer](https://completemusicreadingtrainer.com/)  | The developer describes progressive drills and chapters, user-selected chapters, custom drills/training programs, and several answer inputs including a virtual piano, MIDI, and nearby instrument via microphone.                                      | Let learners choose a mode and practice scope. MIDI and microphone inputs are not implemented in this update.                                          |
| [StaffWars Live](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/staffwars-live/id1071622918) | The App Store listing describes instrument input, a game timer/three lives, saved scores, and notes that speed up after ten correct answers.                                                                                                            | Keep the practice flow focused on learning rather than speed challenges.                                                                                |

## Product decisions

This app offers flashcards, letter choices, piano-key answers, keyboard-to-letter questions, and mixed practice. A five-note round is the default, with 10-note and all-selected-notes options; short pools cycle to fill 5- and 10-note rounds. Wrong answers stay on the current note until correct, and missed questions return later when possible.

## Guided feedback

Sesame Workshop's touch-tablet guide describes progressive wrong-answer support for preschool games, including encouragement and visual guidance. This app uses brief feedback and revisits missed questions. Source: [Sesame Workshop, _Best Practices: Designing Touch Tablet Experiences for Preschoolers_ (PDF)](https://joanganzcooneycenter.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/SesameWorkshop-2012.pdf).
