# Practice mode research

The designs below are based on public descriptions from each product's own site or official app-store listing. They support interaction patterns for note-reading practice; they do not establish that any product is specifically intended for professionals, children, or only one age group.

## Product decisions

This app keeps its existing flashcard experience and adds three answer modes: Guided letters (two or three choices), All letters (A–G), and Piano keys (exact pitch, including octave). A five-note round is the default, with 10-note and all-selected-notes options; short pools cycle to fill 5- and 10-note rounds. Wrong answers stay on the current note until correct. Guided letters adds automatic help after repeated mistakes; the other answer modes provide a manual Hint. A 60-second challenge is available for All letters and Piano keys only; Guided letters stays untimed, and the challenge is off by default.

## Guided feedback

Sesame Workshop's touch-tablet guide describes progressive wrong-answer support for preschool games: first offer encouragement, then add a hint, then highlight the correct choice, repeating until the learner succeeds. This app adapts the escalating part in Guided letters and keeps help available on request in the other answer modes. The guide is specifically about preschool tablet experiences; this is one design reference, not evidence that the app should be age-targeted. Source: [Sesame Workshop, _Best Practices: Designing Touch Tablet Experiences for Preschoolers_ (PDF)](https://joanganzcooneycenter.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/SesameWorkshop-2012.pdf).
